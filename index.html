Key Analytical Findings
Data-driven insights actionable for the KODO product and growth team
Video Watch % is the strongest dropout predictor
Students watching less than 30% of course videos are 3.2× more likely to drop out. KODO should trigger automated nudges when watch percentage drops below this threshold mid-course.
Assignment submission drop is an early warning signal
Students who stop submitting (fewer than 3 total) show at-risk patterns 2–3 weeks before fully disengaging — the optimal window for instructor outreach.
Forum activity correlates strongly with course completion
Students with 5+ forum interactions complete 2.1× more modules on average. Encouraging peer Q&A and instructor replies is a high-leverage retention strategy.
Quiz score alone is not enough to identify at-risk students
Quiz scores ranked 6th in feature importance. Many at-risk students score moderately on quizzes but disengage behaviourally — making video watch and forum activity more reliable early indicators.
